A broadcast lower third for talking-head and interview footage. The accent bar grows down with `scaleY`, the name and title slide in from the left, everything holds, then reverses out.

The full in, hold, out cycle is expressed with `hold(...)` keyframes inside a single `StaggeredMotion` transition per element, so there are no manual frame calculations. Adjust `total`, `enter` and `exit` to fit your shot.
